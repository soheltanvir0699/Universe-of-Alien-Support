UNIVERSE OF ALIEN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Universe of Alien (“the App”), published by Sohel Tanvir / OLL.E Inc, handles information when you use the iOS application.

1. Summary

Gameplay progress is stored on your device. In-app purchases are handled by Apple. The App also shows ads through Google AdMob (rewarded videos and interstitial ads). We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we store on your device

  • Game progress (coins, gems, inventory, planet unlocks, missions, settings)
  • Daily rewarded-ad usage counts (to enforce fair free-reward limits)
  • Optional Keychain / iCloud Keychain backup of save data so progress can restore after reinstall when signed into the same Apple ID

This gameplay data stays under your Apple account / device control and is not uploaded to our own servers.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example gem packs) are processed by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple’s privacy terms also apply to those transactions.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show:

  • Rewarded video ads (for example free shop rewards or free level skips)
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads between some mission stages

Google and its partners may collect and process information to deliver, measure, and improve ads. Depending on your device settings and consent choices, this may include device identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA when available), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device/app information, ad interaction data, and similar technical data.

We do not control Google’s full data practices. Please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data in advertising / partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google’s AdMob help: support.google.com/admob

On iOS, the App may ask for permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads can still appear, but they are typically less personalized.

We do not sell or share personal data with data brokers for money.

5. Analytics

We do not use a separate third-party analytics product beyond what is required for ads and App Store distribution. AdMob may include measurement related to ad delivery and performance.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for our own databases. Advertising partners may process data as described in their policies. Parents who have concerns can contact us (below) and can also use iOS Screen Time / content restrictions.

7. Data retention & deletion

Progress remains until you delete the App or clear app data. Uninstalling removes local UserDefaults data; Keychain items may persist or sync via Apple iCloud Keychain depending on your device settings. To clear progress, delete the App and (if needed) remove related Keychain entries from your Apple ID devices. Advertising identifiers and ad-related data retained by Google are governed by Google’s policies.

8. Your choices

  • Respond to the App Tracking Transparency prompt (Allow or Ask App Not to Track)
  • Change tracking later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Reset your Advertising Identifier in iOS Settings (Apple may relocate this control over time)
  • Use the App without creating an account with us (no developer login required)
  • Manage purchases through your Apple ID / App Store settings
  • Control iCloud Keychain in iOS Settings if you prefer not to sync Keychain items

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
