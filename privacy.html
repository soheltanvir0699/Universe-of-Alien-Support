ASHLINE · UNIVERSE OF ALIEN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ashline (also published as Universe of Alien; “the App”), published by Sohel Tanvir / OLL.E Inc, handles information when you use the App on iOS (App Store) or Android (Google Play). Where something applies to only one platform, it is marked iOS or Android.

1. Summary

You do not need an account to play. Your game progress is stored on your device (and in your own Apple or Google backup). We do not run our own servers for player data and we do not sell your personal information. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. The App shows ads through Google AdMob; Google and its partners may collect device identifiers and related data to show, measure and (with your consent where required) personalize ads.

Store privacy labels: the App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety section describe the same data: Device ID / advertising ID, advertising data and product interaction, approximate location (derived by Google from your IP address), and crash / performance diagnostics. Device ID and advertising data may be used for tracking and third-party advertising when you allow it.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Game progress (coins, gems, lives, levels, inventory, planet unlocks, missions, story progress, settings)
  • Daily rewarded-ad counts and ad pacing timers (to keep ads fair and limited)
  • Free-spin and daily-reward cooldown timestamps
  • iOS: an optional Keychain / iCloud Keychain copy of your save, so progress can return after reinstalling on the same Apple ID
  • Android: your save file may be included in Google’s Android backup for your Google account, so progress can return after reinstalling. Ad pacing data is not backed up.

This gameplay data is not uploaded to our own servers.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example gem packs) are processed by Apple (StoreKit / App Store) on iOS and by Google Play Billing on Android. We never receive your payment card details. Apple’s or Google’s privacy terms apply to those transactions. Gems can be used in the game, including for Lucky Wheel spins, which give random in-game rewards.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show:

  • Rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example free rewards, spins or level help)
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads between some stages
  • App open ads when the App starts or returns from the background (never during a game)
  • Banner ads on some menu screens

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information to deliver, measure, personalize and improve ads. Depending on your device settings and consent choices, this may include:

  • Device ID: the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, or the Android advertising ID on Android, and similar identifiers
  • Advertising data: ads shown, ad interactions and related performance data
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language)
  • Diagnostics related to ad delivery (for example crash and performance data)

Data is sent over encrypted connections. We do not control Google’s data practices; please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • How Google uses data in advertising: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not sell personal data to data brokers.

5. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

On both platforms the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it (for example GDPR in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and privacy laws in some US states). No ads are requested before this step is complete. You can change your choices later from the privacy options in the App when they are available in your region.

6. Tracking on iOS (App Tracking Transparency)

iOS: on iOS 14.5 and later the App may show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing the IDFA. If you tap Allow, Google AdMob and its partners may use your advertising identifier to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized ads and measurement. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, ads still appear but are less personalized and the IDFA is not used for tracking. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

7. Advertising ID on Android

Android: the App (through the AdMob SDK) uses the Android advertising ID for advertising and measurement. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices). Ads continue to appear but are less personalized.

8. Notifications

The App can send optional local reminders (for example when lives have refilled or a daily reward is ready). They are created on your device, contain no personal data, and are only shown if you allow notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your device’s notification settings.

9. Analytics and diagnostics

We do not use a separate third-party analytics product. Ad-related measurement and diagnostics are handled by Google AdMob. Apple and Google may also share anonymous crash reports with us if you have enabled that in your device settings.

10. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load ads, the consent form and purchases
  • Billing (Android): to buy gem packs through Google Play
  • Notifications (optional): for the reminders described above
  • Advertising ID (Android, added by the AdMob SDK) / tracking prompt (iOS)

The App does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help. Parents can use Screen Time (iOS) or Google Family Link (Android) to manage the App.

12. Data retention and deletion

Progress stays on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. iOS: deleting the App removes local data; Keychain items may remain or sync through iCloud Keychain depending on your settings. Android: uninstalling or using “Clear storage” removes local data; you can manage or delete Android backups in your Google account settings. Because we hold no account or server data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; data held by Google for advertising is governed by Google’s policies, and you can reset your advertising identifier at any time.

13. Your choices

  • Accept or refuse consent in the consent form, and change it later in the App’s privacy options
  • iOS: respond to the tracking prompt and change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in device settings
  • Turn notifications on or off in your device settings
  • Play without creating any account with us
  • Manage purchases through your Apple ID or Google Play account

14. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
